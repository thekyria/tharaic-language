# Proverbs & Oaths

[← Overview](../README.md)

Sailors and merchants carry their wisdom in short, fixed sayings. Some are solemn, from the temple and the old chants; most are the dry humor of the harbor. Glosses follow the conventions of the [sample sentences](texts.md).

---

| *Βαλ εσι αχος ϝελοι.* | Bal esi akhos veloi. | Not is brother wind-DAT. | The wind has no brother: a fair wind will not come twice for the asking. |

### Weather sayings

Weather sayings are pairs of phrases with no verb, the sign first and the outcome second.

| Tharaic | Romanized | Gloss | Meaning |
|---------|-----------|-------|---------|
| *Πυρρον ϸαχαρ — ϸα ϝενει σᾱϙιρος.* | Purron shakhar — sha venei sāghiros. | Red dawn — FUT comes storm. | Red dawn: a storm will come. |
| *Πυρρον ϙερεβον — καλος ϝελος.* | Purron gherebon — kalos velos. | Red dusk — fair wind. | Red sky at evening: a fair wind tomorrow. |
| *Λαριμ επι χολελι — σᾱϙιρος επι θᾱρελι.* | Larim epi kholeli — sāghiros epi thāreli. | Gulls on sand-LOC — storm on sea-LOC. | Gulls on the beach, a storm on the sea. |

## Oaths

An oath names what it swears **by** in the **instrumental**: the gods, salt, the ship, or the sea are the means that bind the speaker. Swearing uses *ϸεβεϙειν* "to swear".

**A merchant's oath** over goods and weights:
> *Ϸεβεϙω ηλομις και μαλχομ: εσι μετρον αληθινον, εσι κασαφον καλον.*<br>
> Shebeghō ēlomis kai malkhom: esi metron alēthinon, esi kasaphon kalon.<br>
> I-swear gods-INS and salt-INS: is measure true, is silver good.<br>
> "I swear by the gods and by salt: the measure is true, the silver is good."

**The self-curse** that closes a serious oath:
> *Ιμ λεκω ϸακαρον, λυ ιδει-νι Μᾱτος.*<br>
> Im lekō shakaron, lu idei-ni Mātos.<br>
> If I-speak lie, WISH sees-me Death.<br>
> "If I speak a lie, may Death look upon me."

**A covenant of salt**, an agreement that cannot be broken, like the Hebrew *bərît melaḥ*. It uses the [construct state](morphology.md#construct-state--phoenician-possession):
> *Κεϸερομεν βαρατυλ μαλχον.*<br>
> Kesheromen baratul malkhon.<br>
> We-bind covenant-CONSTR salt.<br>
> "We bind a covenant of salt."

**A rower's oath** to the captain:
> *Ϸεβεϙω ναϝομ και θᾱρομ: βαλ ϸα ϸελεκω ρεμα.*<br>
> Shebeghō navom kai thārom: bal sha shelekō rema.<br>
> I-swear ship-INS and sea-INS: not FUT I-throw oars.<br>
> "I swear by the ship and by the sea: I will not throw down the oars", that is, I will not desert my post.

**Greeting and farewell** in the harbor:

| Tharaic | Romanized | Meaning |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| *Ϸαλαμον!* | Shalamon! | "Peace!", the greeting on meeting, and the word that seals a bargain |
| *Καλος ϝελος!* | Kalos velos! | "Fair wind!", farewell and blessing |
