# Sample Sentences

[← Overview](../README.md)

Short example sentences. For longer texts see [Proverbs & Oaths](proverbs.md), [the Sacred Register](sacred.md) and [Chants & Stories](stories.md).

| Tharaic | Romanized | Gloss | Natural English |
| --------- | ----------- | ------- | ----------------- |
| *Καλος ϝελος!* | Kalos velos! | Fair wind! | Fair winds! (blessing & farewell) |
| *Νᾱϝει ναϝος επι θᾱρελι.* | Nāvei navos epi thāreli. | Sails sailor on sea-LOC. | The sailor sails upon the sea. |
| *Βᾱσιλ ναϝᾱ — αδενιλ θαρια.* | Bāsil navā — adenil tharia. | Captain-CONSTR ship — lord-CONSTR crew. | The ship's captain — lord of the crew. |
| *Ιδει-νι ϳαμος.* | Idei-ni yamos. | Sees-me western-deep. | The deep sea sees me. |
| *Ϝενει ͱῡα ναϝαθα.* | Venei hūa navatha. | Comes he/she ship-ABL. | He comes off the ship. |
| *Φερω-τα ανι ναϝελι.* | Pherō-ta ani naveli. | Carry-you I ship-LOC. | I carry you aboard the ship. |
| *Λακυλ θᾱρος: νᾱϝε, ζᾱε.* | Lakul thāros: nāve, zāe. | Word-CONSTR sea: sail, live. | The law of the sea: sail, live. |
| *Ϸα ιδετον αναιν σεληνᾱν ναϝελι.* | Sha ideton anain selēnān naveli. | FUT see-DU we-two moon ship-LOC. | We two will see the moon from the ship. |
| *Ϝα-ϝενει ϝελος… ϝα-νᾱϝει ναϝᾱ… ϝα-λεκει βᾱσιλος.* | Va-venei velos… va-nāvei navā… va-lekei bāsilos. | And-comes wind… and-sails ship… and-speaks captain. | The wind came… the ship sailed… the captain spoke. |
| *Λεκω ανι — ανι, ναϝος θᾱρι!* | Lekō ani — ani, navos thāri! | Speak I — I, sailor of-deep! | I speak — I, sailor of the deep! |
| *Ϸα νᾱϝουσι καπανιμ μιζραχᾱθα.* | Sha nāvousi kapanim mizrakhātha. | FUT sail merchants east-ABL. | The merchants will sail in from the east. |
| *Ιμ μη ϝενει ϝελος, βαλ ϸα νᾱϝω.* | Im mē venei velos, bal sha nāvō. | If NEG comes wind, NEG FUT I-sail. | Unless the wind comes, I will not sail. |
| *Ͱα-εσι ναϝᾱ εν λιμελι? — Ναι.* | Ha-esi navā en limeli? — Nai. | Q-is ship in harbor-LOC? — Yes. | Is there a ship in the harbor? — Yes. |
| *Μα φερει ναϝᾱ ϸε-ϝενει μιζραχᾱθα?* | Ma pherei navā she-venei mizrakhātha? | What carries ship that-comes east-ABL? | What does the ship that comes from the east carry? |
| *Εσι θᾱρος μεγοτερος ναϝαθα.* | Esi thāros megoteros navatha. | Is sea bigger ship-ABL. | The sea is bigger than the ship. |
| *Φερεται ναϝᾱ ϝελαθα προς λιμᾱν.* | Pheretai navā velatha pros limān. | Is-carried ship wind-ABL toward harbor-ACC. | The ship is carried toward the harbor by the wind. |
| *Εκανε νᾱϝει αδενος δια θᾱρον.* | Ekane nāvei adenos dia thāron. | Was sails lord across sea-ACC. | The lord used to sail across the sea. |
| *Ιμ εκανε ϝελος καλος, αν ενᾱϝαμεν.* | Im ekane velos kalos, an enāvamen. | If was wind fair, would we-sailed. | If the wind had been fair, we would have sailed. |
| *Μη ιδει-νι θᾱρος!* | Mē idei-ni thāros! | NEG sees-me sea! | May the sea not see me! |
| *Εσι ναϝᾱ βᾱσιλοι — ναϝιτ ναϝιμ.* | Esi navā bāsiloi — navit navim. | Is ship captain-DAT — ship-CONSTR ships. | The captain has a ship — the ship of ships. |
